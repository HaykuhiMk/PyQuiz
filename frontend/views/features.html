Features

Everything you can do in PyQuiz today. Features marked "Account" need you to be signed in.

Quizzes

Three quiz modes

Classic at your own pace, Blitz with 45 seconds per question, and Survival, where one mistake ends the run.

Topics and difficulty

Search and pick the topics you want, group them by category, and filter by easy, medium or hard.

Randomized questions

Questions are drawn at random from the topics you choose, so each session plays differently.

Explanations

Questions include explanations of the Python behaviour behind the answer.

Guest mode

Play quizzes without signing up. Your progress isn't saved while you're a guest.

Learning and progress

Study mode

Browse questions with their code, answers and explanations, filtered by topic and difficulty.

Open study mode
Account

Daily challenge

Five questions, the same for everyone each day. Submit once and earn 20 bonus points for every correct answer.

Account

Progress dashboard

Your points, accuracy and streak, topic mastery with your weakest topics, and the achievements you've unlocked.

Global leaderboard

The top 50 players, ranked by total points and then by best streak. Signed-in players see their own position highlighted.

See the leaderboard
Account

Achievements

Five milestones: your first correct answer, streaks of 5 and 10, and 100 and 500 points.

Light and dark themes

Switch themes from the top bar. PyQuiz follows your system setting until you choose.

Not available yet

PyQuiz doesn't have friend lists or private leaderboards, and it doesn't keep a day-by-day history of your activity. Planned work is tracked in the project's GitHub issues.
